CadQuarry vs. other CAD / geometry datasets

An apples-to-apples look at CadQuarry beside the datasets most used for geometric deep learning and CAD generation. Each section below renders a sample of ~100 real models, live in your browser — click any to spin it in 3D.

Licensing — read this. Only the CadQuarry models are CC0 and ours to share freely. The third-party samples are hosted here for visual comparison only; they are not CC0 and each retains its origin dataset's / author's license (often per-model and inconsistent). ShapeNet and DeepCAD are not shown as meshes — ShapeNet's surface meshes are gated behind a signed agreement, and DeepCAD ships command-sequences, not meshes. Full per-source detail and the attribution table are in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.
