How CadQuarry compares to other CAD/geometry datasets

An apples-to-apples look at CadQuarry next to the datasets most often used for geometric deep learning and CAD generation. The CadQuarry parts below are live, interactive 3D — they are public-domain (CC0) and ours to share. Spin them, then read how the alternatives differ.

About the other datasets: we deliberately do not re-host their models here. Several (ShapeNet, Fusion 360 Gallery) are distributed under terms that forbid redistribution outright; others (ABC, DeepCAD, Thingi10K) carry per-model, inconsistent licenses, so "the dataset is for research" never establishes that any single model may be re-hosted. Each card links to the dataset's own distribution. That gap — needing to chase down per-model rights — is precisely the friction CadQuarry removes. See THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md for the full breakdown.
